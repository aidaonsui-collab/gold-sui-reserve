The Gold Reserve

A gold-miner skill game on Sui. Trading fees buy real tokenized gold, holders can redeem for it, and players stake to build mines and compete each round.

⛏️ Play the demoSimulated tokens, no wallet, nothing on-chain.
Coming soon on vicefun.com Status: scoping. No contracts are deployed yet.
🏦

Gold vault

The token's trading-fee share is swapped into XAUm (Matrixdock tokenized gold) and deposited in an on-chain vault.

🔁

Redeemable

Holders can redeem $RESERVE for their pro-rata share of the vault's XAUm. A small redemption fee stays in the vault, raising backing for everyone who holds.

⛏️

Stake to build

Lock $RESERVE to place mines and shafts on a 16×16 grid in timed rounds. Stakes come back after the round. A fee-funded rewards pot pays out by score.
