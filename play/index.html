    How scoring works
    • Mine score = richness × vein × crowding × shaft × time.
    • Vein: +20% per orthogonally adjacent mine of yours, max +60%.
    • Crowding: −15% per mine (anyone's) beyond 3 in its 3×3.
    • Shaft: +25% per Vault Shaft within 2 cells (max 3), and that includes rivals' shafts boosting your mines and yours boosting theirs.
    • Time: a mine placed with half the round left scores half. Moving restarts its clock.
    • Each extra building of a type costs +25% stake (caps: 24 mines, 6 shafts). First placer owns a cell.
    • Survey (40, a fee into the prize pool) reveals exact richness in a 5×5 area. Otherwise you only see an estimate.
    • At close the prize pool splits pro-rata by score. Stakes stay locked until you demolish or rebuild.
